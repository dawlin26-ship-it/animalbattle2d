Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy covers Animal Battle 2D Uploader (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Animal Battle 2D YouTube channel (@AnimalBattle2D). The App is used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the channel's own videos to its own channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and confirm that videos go only to the Animal Battle 2D channel.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the channel's own videos. The App does not access any other user's account or data.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tokens from the owner's computer also ends the App's access.

Deleting your data

The App keeps no data about anyone other than the channel owner. To delete everything it stored, remove the App's access as described above and delete the tokens and logs from the owner's computer, or email the contact address below with the subject "Delete my data" and it will be removed within 30 days.
